The bakery on the corner

Made from scratch, the old-fashioned way

A family-run bakery and café in the heart of Camperdown. Our loaves are shaped by hand and baked fresh each day, and most of what's on the counter — from cakes to pies to sauces — is made in-house, with local produce wherever we can.
