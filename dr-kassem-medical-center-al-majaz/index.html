FAQ

Questions patients ask

What dental services do you offer?

We offer preventive care (check-ups and cleanings), restorative treatment such as fillings and root canals, cosmetic dentistry, orthodontics and emergency dental care.

How do I book an appointment?

Call the clinic on +971 6 527 7091, message us on WhatsApp at +971 50 573 3004, or send the appointment request form above and we will contact you to confirm.

Do you accept insurance?

Yes, we accept most major insurance plans. Please contact us with your provider's name and we will confirm the details.

What happens at my first visit?

We carry out a comprehensive dental examination and then talk you through your treatment options before anything is done.

Do you treat dental emergencies?

Yes. If you have severe pain, swelling or a broken tooth, please call us immediately so we can see you as soon as possible.
