Welcome

Tea, cake and a warm welcome on High Street

Tucked into Bridlington's historic Old Town, The Georgian Tearooms is a relaxed place to stop for a pot of tea and something homemade, whether you're starting your morning, meeting friends for lunch or treating yourself to afternoon tea.

Everything is served at the table, with cakes, scones and sweet treats made in-house. Once you're done, take a look around the antiques upstairs.
