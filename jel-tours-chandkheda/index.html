FAQ

Good to know

Can you customise a package for my family or group?

Yes. Every itinerary can be changed — add or remove destinations, change hotels, or adjust the number of days to suit your group.

When is the best time to visit the Rann of Kutch?

The white desert is best seen in the winter months, when Rann Utsav is held. Stays fill up early, so it helps to enquire well in advance.

Do you arrange only hotels or only a car?

Yes. You can book a hotel, a car rental or an airport/railway transfer on its own, without taking a full package.

Do you handle corporate and conference travel?

Yes — group movements, incentives, conferences and VIP travel are handled by the same ground team.

How do I get a quote?

Send the enquiry form, WhatsApp us, or call the 24-hour helpline with your dates, number of travellers and places you'd like to see.
