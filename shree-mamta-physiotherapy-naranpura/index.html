What we do

Care that gets you moving again

Every plan starts with a proper assessment, then a step-by-step programme you can actually follow — in the clinic and at home.

Pain Relief & Rehabilitation

For back and neck pain, joint stiffness, sports strains and recovery after surgery or injury.

  • Joint & soft-tissue mobilisation
  • Heat and cold therapy
  • Gait & walking-aid training
Fees: price on request

Weight Loss & Diet Guidance

Practical, home-food-friendly diet plans paired with safe exercise for steady, lasting results.

  • Personal diet consultation
  • Exercise plan matched to your body
  • Regular progress check-ins
Fees: price on request

Posture, Strength & Mobility

Build strength and flexibility, correct posture and keep joints moving freely as you age.

  • Postural training
  • Range-of-motion exercise
  • Home-care physiotherapy visits
Fees: price on request
