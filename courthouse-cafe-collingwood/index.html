Welcome

Good food in a building with a story

Set in Collingwood's old courthouse at the top of Golden Bay, the café is an easy stop for locals and travellers heading to Farewell Spit. Grab a table inside or out in the sun, order a coffee and something from the cabinet, and take your time.
