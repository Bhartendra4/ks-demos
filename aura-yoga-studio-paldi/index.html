What we teach

Yoga for every body, every age

Small, guided batches where each student gets attention — whether you are stepping on the mat for the first time or deepening an existing practice.

Classic Hatha postures and flowing Ashtanga Vinyasa sequences to build strength, balance and flexibility at your own pace.
