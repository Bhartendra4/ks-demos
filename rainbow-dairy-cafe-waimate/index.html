About us

A proper local dairy, with a cafe inside.

Rainbow Dairy & Cafe is a great place to come and eat, have a coffee or a cold drink, then get on with your day. Whether you're a local on your lunch break or passing through Waimate, we'll look after you.

  • Sandwiches, rolls and salads made fresh every day
  • Hot food, toasties and mini pizzas made in-store
  • Coffee, cold drinks and big ice creams
  • A small selection of everyday grocery items
