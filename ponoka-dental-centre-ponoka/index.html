FAQ

Common questions

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes — we're welcoming new patients and families. Send an appointment request above or call (403) 783-5844.

What are your office hours?

We're open Monday to Thursday, 8:00 am to 4:30 pm, and closed Friday through Sunday. You can send an online request any time and we'll reply during office hours.

Do you see children?

Yes. We offer family dentistry for patients of all ages, so you can book your children's visits with the same clinic as your own.

What should I bring to my first visit?

Please bring your dental insurance details, a list of any medications you take, and your completed new-patient form if you filled it in ahead of time.

Do you bill my insurance directly?

Please call the clinic with your insurance details and our team will explain how billing works for your plan.

Where are you located?

We're at 5012 48 Avenue, Ponoka, Alberta T4J 1R7. Tap "Get directions" above to open the route in your maps app.
