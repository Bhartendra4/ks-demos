Bienvenue

A little corner of France in SW18

Bakery Lamour is a neighbourhood French bakery on Huguenot Place, Wandsworth. We bake sourdough loaves, French baguettes, croissants and pastries, and pour coffee to enjoy in or take away.

Pop in for breakfast on the way to the station, pick up a loaf for the weekend, or order a box of pastries for the office.
