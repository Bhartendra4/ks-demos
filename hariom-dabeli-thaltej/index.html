About us

Your neighbourhood dabeli stop in Thaltej

Hariom Dabeli is a small, friendly snack counter on Sattadhar Cross Road, serving Kutchi dabeli and quick bites to families, students and office-goers in Thaltej, Sola and Ghatlodia.

  • Dabeli made to order, in oil or butter
  • Open from morning till late night, all 7 days
  • Quick takeaway, so call ahead and pick up
  • Drinking water and hand-wash available
