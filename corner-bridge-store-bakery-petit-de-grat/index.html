Our story

A family corner store since the 1950s

Corner Bridge started out in the early 1950s as a small convenience store in Petit de Grat. Over the years the family grew it into the bakery and store it is today — still family-run, still the friendly stop by the bridge for neighbours and visitors to Isle Madame.

Stop in for fresh baking, a meal to take home, your mail, or a can of paint for the weekend project.
