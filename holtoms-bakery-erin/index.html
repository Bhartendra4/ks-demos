Our Story

A family bakery on Main Street

Holtom's is a third-generation, family-run bakery. We still bake the old-fashioned way: from scratch, with recipes handed down in the family.

  • Everything made from scratch, no shortcuts
  • Family recipes passed down through three generations
  • Breads, cookies, pastries, donuts and pies
  • Right in the heart of downtown Erin
