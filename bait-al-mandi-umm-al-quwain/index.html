Our kitchen

Mandi, made the patient way

Mandi is all about time: tender, gently spiced meat cooked over long-grain rice so every grain soaks up the flavour. It's a meal made for sharing — on a big platter, around the floor, with the whole table.

  • 1Mandi & madhbi — chicken or meat over spiced rice, cooked fresh through the day.
  • 2Haneeth — slow-roasted, fall-apart tender, for bigger appetites and gatherings.
  • 3Majlis-style seating — sit back the traditional way, or take it home for the family.
