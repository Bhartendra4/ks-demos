Ophthalmologists and an optometrist working together, so you can be examined, diagnosed, treated and fitted for glasses in one place.

Cataract Surgery

When glasses no longer help, modern cataract surgery replaces the cloudy lens with a clear lens implant through a very small incision.

  • No-stitch, no-patch, no-shot technique
  • Outpatient procedure

Glaucoma Care

Glaucoma often has no early symptoms. Regular checks catch it early, and treatment can protect your sight.

  • Eye pressure & optic nerve checks
  • Medicated drops
  • In-office laser trabeculoplasty

FAQ

Common questions

How do I know if I have a cataract?

Early cataracts may cause slightly blurred or cloudy vision, extra glare from lamps or headlights at night, and colors that look faded. An eye exam is the only way to know for sure.

What does "no-stitch, no-patch, no-shot" cataract surgery mean?

The cataract is removed through a very small incision that usually seals on its own, without stitches, an eye patch or an injection around the eye. Your surgeon will explain what to expect at your visit.

Can glaucoma be treated?

Yes. Detected early, glaucoma can usually be controlled with eye drops. If drops are not enough, in-office laser treatment or surgery may be options.

I'm a new patient. Can I register before my visit?

Yes. New patients can complete registration forms online before their appointment to shorten the wait at the clinic.
