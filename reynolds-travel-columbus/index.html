Good to know

Frequently asked questions

Do your game-day packages include game tickets?

Packages are built around the game — ask us which options include tickets, hotel and transfers for the trip you're interested in.

Can you plan a trip for our club or alumni group?

Yes. Group travel is a core part of what we do — tell us your group size, dates and budget and we'll put together a proposal.

Do I have to fly, or are land-only packages available?

Many trips are offered both air-inclusive and land-only, so you can use your own flights or miles if you prefer.

What are escorted tours?

A professional tour director travels with the group the whole way — hotels, sightseeing, transport and many meals are pre-arranged.

How do I hold a spot on an upcoming trip?

Send a trip request or call the office. Your agent will confirm availability and explain deposit and payment steps.
