FAQ

Common questions

Can't find your answer? Call or WhatsApp the hospital during OPD hours.

Do I need an appointment, or can I walk in?

Walk-ins are welcome during OPD hours (Mon–Sat, 10 AM – 8 PM). Booking ahead helps reduce your waiting time.

How do I know if my cataract needs surgery?

If blurred or cloudy vision is affecting daily tasks like reading, driving or recognising faces, a consultation will tell you whether surgery is the right time. The doctor will examine your eyes and explain the options.

Am I suitable for LASIK?

Suitability depends on your prescription stability, corneal thickness and eye health. A detailed pre-LASIK evaluation is done before any recommendation.

I have diabetes. How often should I get my eyes checked?

People with diabetes should have a retina examination regularly, as advised by the doctor, even if vision seems normal — early changes often have no symptoms.

At what age should children have their first eye test?

An eye check is a good idea before a child starts school, or sooner if you notice squinting, eye-rubbing, sitting too close to screens or one eye turning.
