About the clinic

A neighbourhood pet clinic for Chandkheda families

Dr.A.P's Pet Clinic looks after dogs and cats at every stage of life — from a puppy's or kitten's first vaccines to regular check-ups, diagnosis and treatment when your pet is unwell.

We take time to explain what's going on with your pet and what the treatment involves, so you can make the right decision with confidence.

Routine check-ups Vaccinations Diagnosis Treatment Preventive care

Visiting us is easy

  1. Message or call on WhatsApp / phone to check the doctor's availability.
  2. Bring your pet on a leash or in a carrier, along with any old vaccination or medical records.
  3. Get a clear plan — check-up, vaccines or treatment, explained step by step.
