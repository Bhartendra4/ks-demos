About the academy

A home for Indian classical music in Paldi

Pancham Academy of Indian Music welcomes students of every age who want to explore the rich tradition of Indian classical music — whether you are starting your very first sa re ga ma or returning to riyaz after years.

1

Step-by-step learning. Swar, taal and raag introduced in a clear, structured order.

2

All ages welcome. Batches for children, students and working adults.

3

Regular riyaz. Practice routines you can continue at home between classes.
