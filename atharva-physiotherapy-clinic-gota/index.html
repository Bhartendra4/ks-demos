Treatments

Care for pain, injury and recovery

From a stiff back to recovery after surgery or a stroke — every programme starts with a proper assessment.

Orthopedic Rehabilitation

Back, neck, knee and shoulder pain, fractures and post-operative recovery — restoring strength and movement step by step.

Sports Physiotherapy

Sprains, strains and overuse injuries — treatment, taping and a graded plan to get you back to your sport safely.

Geriatric Physiotherapy

Gentle programmes for balance, joint stiffness and everyday mobility, helping seniors stay active and independent.

Neurological Rehabilitation

Support after stroke and for nerve-related conditions — working on balance, coordination and daily function.

Pediatric Physiotherapy

Child-friendly sessions for developmental delays and movement difficulties, with guidance for parents.

Cardiopulmonary Rehab

Breathing exercises and graded activity to improve stamina and lung function after illness or surgery.

FAQ

Common questions

Do I need a doctor's referral?

You can book directly. If you have a doctor's note, X-ray or MRI report, please bring it to your first visit.

What should I wear?

Comfortable, loose clothing that lets the therapist assess and treat the affected area easily.

How many sessions will I need?

It depends on your condition. After the first assessment you will get a clear idea of the plan and expected number of sessions.

Do you treat children and senior citizens?

Yes — the clinic offers pediatric and geriatric physiotherapy with programmes suited to each age group.

How do I book?

Call or WhatsApp +91 63540 73970, email the clinic, or use the appointment form on this page.
