Welcome

A friendly neighbourhood yoga studio near Krishnabaug

Nidhi's Yogahub is a yoga studio in Maninagar, Ahmedabad, offering regular group classes for beginners through to advanced students, along with focused sessions for weight management, meditation and kids' fitness.

Classes run in batches from early morning to evening, six days a week — so you can fit your practice around work, college or school.

Beginners welcome Morning & evening batches Kids & adults Air-conditioned studio

What you can practise

  • Asanas for flexibility, strength and posture
  • Pranayama — breathing techniques for energy and calm
  • Meditation and Yoga Nidra for deep relaxation
  • 108 Surya Namaskar sessions
  • Yoga and fun fitness activities for kids
