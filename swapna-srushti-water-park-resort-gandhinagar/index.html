FAQ

Good to know before you come

How do I check ticket prices and timings?

Rates and timings can change by season and day of the week. Send the enquiry form or call us and the team will share the current details for your date.

Can we visit the water park and stay at the resort on the same trip?

Yes. Both are on the same campus, so you can spend the day at the park and stay overnight. Mention “Resort stay” in your enquiry and we will plan both together.

Do you take school picnics and corporate groups?

Yes, group visits are welcome. Share your group size and preferred date and we will get back with a plan for your group.

Can I book the venue for a wedding or a shoot?

The party plot and garden areas are available for events, and the campus is open for pre-wedding and film shoots. Enquire with your date for availability.

Is food available on campus?

Yes, there is a restaurant on campus. For large groups and events, let us know your requirements in advance.
