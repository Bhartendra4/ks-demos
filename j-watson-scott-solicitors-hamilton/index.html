FAQ

Common questions

Which areas of law do you cover?

We advise on wills, executry, trusts, estate planning, powers of attorney and guardianship; residential and commercial conveyancing; and family law including divorce, child custody, cohabitation, adoption and civil partnerships.

Will I deal with a solicitor directly?

Yes. From the outset you will have face-to-face contact with your solicitor, who will look after your matter personally.

Which areas do you serve?

Our office is in Hamilton and we act for clients throughout Lanarkshire, including Motherwell, Airdrie and Coatbridge.

How do I find out about fees?

Please get in touch and we will explain the likely costs for your matter before any work begins.

How do I make an appointment?

Call 01698 282370, email mail@watsonscott.co.uk, or use the enquiry form above and we will arrange a time that suits you.
