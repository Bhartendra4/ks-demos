Our cafe

In the heart of Nobber village

The Greenways Cafe sits on Main Street, just a minute from the Boyne Valley to Lakelands County Greenway. Whether you're cycling the route, out for a walk or meeting friends, it's an easy place to stop, sit down and enjoy good local food.
