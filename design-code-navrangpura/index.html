The Studio

Architecture and interiors under one roof — since 1994.

We offer contemporary, timeless and sustainable design across architecture, urban design, planning, interior design and project management. Our team takes a project from the first sketch to the finished space, so the building and its interiors are conceived together.

Every design starts with careful study of the site, the brief and the budget, and brings in natural light, materials and Vastu principles wherever the client wants them.

FAQ

Questions clients often ask.

Do you handle both architecture and interior design?

Yes. The studio offers architecture, interior design, landscape, urban planning and project management, so one team can take a project from plan to finished interiors.

What kinds of projects do you take on?

Private residences such as bungalows, farm houses and weekend homes, apartments, corporate offices, shopping centres, hotels, institutional buildings and industrial plants.

Can the design follow Vastu?

Yes — Vastu principles and natural elements can be built into the planning from the start if you would like them.

Do you work outside Ahmedabad?

Please share your project location in the enquiry form or call the studio, and the team will let you know.

How do I get started?

Fill in the enquiry form, call, or send a WhatsApp message. The team will set up a first meeting to understand your requirements and budget.
