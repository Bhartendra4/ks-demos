Our café

A friendly stop in the heart of Trim

Stonewall Coffee opened its doors on Castle Street in June 2024, just across the road from Trim Tourist Office and the castle itself. Whether you're a local on the way to work or visiting one of Ireland's great Norman castles, we're here to fuel your day.

  • Freshly brewed coffee, every cup
  • Healthy options like acai bowls
  • Breakfast served until 11am
  • Indoor & outdoor seating, or take away
