Welcome

A Valley landmark, lovingly revived

The Bridgetown Motor Inn began as a private residence in 1956 and grew into a motor inn in the early seventies. Today, under new management, it’s being refreshed room by room — with the same quiet setting guests have always loved.

  • Air-conditioned rooms

FAQ

Good to know before you arrive

Are you open in winter?

Yes — the Bridgetown Motor Inn is open year round.

What’s in the rooms?

Every room has air conditioning, a flat-screen TV with cable, Wi-Fi, a mini fridge and a microwave. Hair dryers, cribs and cots are available on request.

Do you have an accessible room?

Yes, we have a fully accessible room with one queen bed and one single bed.

Is there laundry on site?

Yes. Our guest laundry room has a coin-operated washer and dryer, an iron and ironing board, and an ice machine.

What is your cancellation policy?

We ask for 72 hours’ notice for cancellations. A credit card or deposit is required to hold a room.

Can I smoke at the property?

Smoking is permitted in designated areas only.
