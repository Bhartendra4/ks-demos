Our story

A cosy corner of Trowbridge, made with care

Sweet Bramble is a family-run café where everything is about warm welcomes and good homemade food. Pop in for a hearty breakfast, a relaxed brunch with friends, or a quiet coffee and something sweet.

Our menu brings together the comfort of British café classics and the baking traditions of Ukraine, with seasonal specialities made by our own pastry chef.
