Our kitchen

All kinds of South Indian food, made fresh

From breakfast dosa to a full biryani lunch and evening grills, Humaira is the neighbourhood cafeteria for a quick, filling meal at an honest price.

  • 1Biryani & curries — chicken biryani, chicken curry and mutton dishes.
  • 2Porotta specials — chicken and beef kuttu porotta, murtaba, ghee roast.
  • 3Grills & fresh fish — grill chicken, mutton grills and fish of the day.
  • 4Juices & tea — fresh juices, mojitos and tea anytime.
