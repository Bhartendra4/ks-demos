FAQ

Common questions

Do you offer fixed fees?

Yes, for many routine matters such as straightforward wills, uncontested divorces, lasting powers of attorney, conveyancing and debt collection letters. Contact us for a quotation.

Can I deal with you without coming into the office?

Yes. You can work with us in person, by phone, by letter or by email — whichever you prefer.

Can you visit me at home or in hospital?

Where necessary, home or hospital visits can be arranged. Please call your nearest office to discuss.

Are your offices accessible?

Both offices have ground-floor interview rooms. Mildenhall has private client parking, and there is public parking within easy walking distance of our Newmarket office.

My matter isn't listed — can you still help?

Our list of services isn't exhaustive. Please get in touch anyway and we'll let you know how we can help.
