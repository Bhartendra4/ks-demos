Welcome in

A proper cuppa and something homemade

A mother-and-daughter team runs The Cosy Tearoom inside Dore Moor Garden Centre on the edge of Sheffield. Drop in after a wander round the plants for a pot of tea, a slice of cake or a warming home-cooked lunch.

  • Cakes baked with care, from classic sponges to seasonal bakes
  • Savouries and home-cooked food for breakfast and lunch
  • In-store collection: order ahead and pick up
  • Special events such as themed afternoon teas with live music
