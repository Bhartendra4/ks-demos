Our Story

A family bakery on Main Avenue

The Bakery on Main is a women-owned, family-run bakery in the heart of Ashtabula. We believe good baking is simple: real ingredients, made by hand, baked fresh.

  • Everything made from scratch in our own kitchen
  • Fresh ingredients used daily
  • Croissants, cookies, cinnamon rolls, breads and more
