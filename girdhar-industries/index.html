About Us

A copper wire plant built around consistency.

Girdhar Industries is a copper winding wire manufacturing unit serving transformer and motor OEMs, pump and fan makers, auto component suppliers, rewinding workshops and traders.

Every stage happens under one roof: drawing the copper rod down to size, annealing it for softness, applying the enamel or paper covering, testing each lot in our lab and packing it moisture-safe on the spool you need.

  • Electrolytic tough pitch (ETP) copper rod, 99.9% Cu minimum
  • In-house drawing, annealing, enamelling, paper taping and testing
  • Grade 1, 2 and 3 insulation build, thermal class 130 to 220
  • Custom sizes, spool types and labelling for OEM lines
  • Lot-wise test certificate and traceable batch number on every spool

Manufacturing Process

From copper rod to finished spool.

Seven controlled stages, all in-house, so every spool carries the same quality.

01

Copper rod

8 mm ETP copper rod from approved producers, checked for conductivity and surface.

02

Wire drawing

Rod breakdown and fine drawing through tungsten carbide and diamond dies to exact size.

03

Annealing

Controlled in-line annealing for softness, elongation and easy winding.

04

Enamelling / taping

Multi-pass enamel coating and curing, or paper taping for PICC conductors.

05

Testing

Lab checks on every lot: size, elongation, breakdown voltage, pin-holes, resistance.

06

Spooling

Precision take-up on DIN / PT spools with label, size, class and batch number.

07

Packing & dispatch

Moisture-safe wrapping, cartons or pallets, and pan-India transport.

Ask on WhatsApp
What is the minimum order quantity?

Stock sizes can be supplied in small quantities for workshops. For special sizes and OEM orders, MOQ depends on the size and enamel type. sample answer, confirm with factory

How is the price decided?

Copper wire is priced on the prevailing copper rate on the day of order, plus a conversion charge that depends on size, enamel class and packing. We share both parts clearly in the quotation.

Which sizes and thermal classes do you make?

Round enamelled wire roughly from SWG 8 to SWG 44, rectangular strip by thickness and width, and thermal classes 130, 155, 180, 200 and 220. See the specification sheet above or send us your drawing.

Do you give a test certificate?

Yes. Each lot is tested in our lab and a test certificate with diameter, elongation, breakdown voltage and resistance results is sent with the consignment.

Can I get a sample before a bulk order?

Yes, samples can be arranged for approval on your winding machine before bulk production.

What spools and packing do you use?

DIN and PT type plastic spools for enamelled wire, wooden drums or coils for strips and bare wire, all wrapped against moisture and labelled with size, class and batch number.

How long does delivery take?

Stock sizes are typically dispatched within 48 hours. Made-to-order items take a few days depending on quantity. We deliver across India through transport partners. sample answer
