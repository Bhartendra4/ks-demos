About us

Baked in Fergus, made to order

Cinnastir's Bake Shop is a small online bake shop in Fergus, Ontario. We bake to order, so your treats are fresh when you pick them up.

  • Everything is made in house
  • The best ingredients we can get, with nothing artificial
  • All items are available by pre-order
