Questions

How is the fare calculated?

Round trip: kilometres × rate, with a minimum of 300 km per day, plus driver allowance per day. One way: distance × rate plus driver allowance. Toll, parking and state permit are extra at actual.

Do I pay advance?

A small advance confirms the booking. Pay the rest to the driver after the trip. We never ask for payment through this website.

Can I book for a wedding?

Yes. Tell us the date, number of cars and pickup points on WhatsApp and we will send a combined quote.
