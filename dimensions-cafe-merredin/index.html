About us

A local café on Bates Street

Dimensions Café is a relaxed spot in Merredin for locals and travellers alike. Pull up a chair for breakfast, catch up over coffee and cake, or drop in for lunch.

  • Full breakfasts, coffee, cakes and lunches
  • EFTPOS available
  • Easy stop in Merredin town centre
