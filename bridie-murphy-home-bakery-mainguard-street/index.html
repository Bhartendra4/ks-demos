Our bakery

A little home bakery in Galway's old town

You'll find us on Mainguard Street in the heart of Galway city centre. We bake the comforting things people come back for: scones, breads and cakes made the home-baked way.

Stop by for breakfast on the go, a coffee and a treat in the afternoon, or something sweet to bring home.

  • Freshly baked goods every day we open
  • Coffee, tea and something to go with it
  • Cakes for birthdays and occasions on request
