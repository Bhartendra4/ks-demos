FAQ

Questions patients ask

Are you accepting new patients?

Yes — new patients are welcome. Request an appointment online or call (515) 964-5700 and we'll find a time that works for you.

What if I don't have dental insurance?

You can still get the care you need. Ask our team about the options available for patients without insurance.

Do you see children and adults?

Yes. We provide comprehensive dental care for the entire family, from routine cleanings to cosmetic and restorative treatment.

I get nervous at the dentist. Can you help?

Absolutely. We offer sedation and comfort options, and our treatment rooms have televisions to help you relax.

What are your office hours?

Monday to Thursday, 7:30am to 4:00pm. Friday visits are available by appointment only.
