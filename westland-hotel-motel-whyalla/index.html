FAQ

Good to know

How do I book a room?

Use the "Book a room" button to check live availability online, or call reception on (08) 8645 0066.

Is breakfast included with accommodation?

Yes — accommodation includes breakfast and Wi-Fi. Ask reception about current room types and rates.

How many guests can your function rooms hold?

Our spaces range from small meeting rooms for around 15 people up to the Westland Convention Room, which holds up to 900.

Can you cater for weddings and formal dinners?

Yes. The Palms Venue and Convention Room are set up for weddings, formal dinners and trade shows, with catering from our own kitchen. Send a function enquiry and we'll talk through menus and layouts.

Do I need to book a table at the Vegas Restaurant?

Bookings are recommended, especially on weekends. Call (08) 8645 0066 to reserve a table.

Is there parking on site?

Yes, free on-site parking is available for hotel and motel guests.
