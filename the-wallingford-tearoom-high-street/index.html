About the tearoom

A hidden gem on the first floor

Nestled upstairs in the historic Wallingford Arcade — formerly The Lamb — the tearoom is a calm spot to pause between browsing the antiques below and exploring the town.

Run by Phil and Laura, it's a place for a relaxed breakfast, an unhurried lunch or a celebratory afternoon tea. Enter through the arcade's main or rear doors.
