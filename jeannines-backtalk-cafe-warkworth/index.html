Welcome in

A Main Street café made for catching up

Jeannine's Backtalk Café is a friendly spot in the heart of Warkworth — the kind of place where you pull up a chair, order something good and talk a while.

Drop in for a coffee and something fresh from the kitchen, meet friends for lunch, or let us look after the food for your next gathering.
