FAQ

Questions parents ask

What are the school timings?

Pre-Nursery and Nursery: 9:00 am – 11:30 am. Junior KG and Senior KG: 9:00 am – 12:30 pm. Classes run Monday to Friday, excluding holidays.

What age should my child be?

Nursery is for children aged 3 years and above, Junior KG for 4 years and above, and Senior KG for 5 years and above.

Is there an entrance test or interview?

No. There is no formal test or interview for the child. Admission is on a first-come, first-served basis after a conversation with the parents.

What is the teacher–child ratio?

Lakshaya Preschool keeps a low teacher–child ratio of 1:10.

Do I need to send a lunch box?

No lunch boxes are required. Nutritious meals are provided during school hours, and special diets are available for children with food allergies.

Is there a uniform?

Yes, children wear a specially designed school uniform.

How do I find out about fees?

Please call or visit the school office for details of the fee structure.
