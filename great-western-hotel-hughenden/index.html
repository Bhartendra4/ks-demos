Getting here

  • Hughenden sits between Charters Towers and Richmond.
  • About 400 km inland of Townsville.
  • A natural overnight stop for travellers heading from the north-east coast towards Alice Springs and Uluru.

Hours

  • Restaurant: lunch and dinner, Monday to Saturday.
  • Bar: open till late.
Good to know

Frequently asked questions

How do I book a room?

Send the enquiry form above or call 07 4741 1454. The hotel will confirm availability and the rate with you directly.

Is there parking?

Yes. Free parking is available on site.

Can I get a meal at the hotel?

Yes. The restaurant is open for lunch and dinner from Monday to Saturday, and the bar is open till late.

Is there a bottle shop?

Yes, there is a bottle shop at the hotel.

Is the hotel accessible?

Disabled access is available. Please contact the hotel before you arrive so the team can explain what suits your needs.
