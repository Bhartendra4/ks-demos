The rule

  • Pay Micro & Small vendors (manufacturers and service providers) within 15 days, or 45 days at most with a written agreement.
  • If the limit has passed and the amount is still unpaid at year end, it is not deductible that year (Section 43B(h)).
  • Late payments also carry MSMED interest at 3× the RBI bank rate, compounded monthly and not deductible.
  • Companies must report these dues in MSME Form 1 (half-yearly) and Form 3CD Clause 22.
