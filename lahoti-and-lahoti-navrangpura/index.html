FAQ

Common questions

Which documents should I bring for my income tax return?

Usually PAN, Aadhaar, Form 16 or income details, bank statements, investment and deduction proofs, and last year's return if available. We'll confirm the exact list when you call.

Can I file my return without visiting the office?

Yes. Documents can be shared online and we can discuss your return over a call or WhatsApp.

I received an income tax notice. What should I do?

Don't ignore it. Share a copy with us and we'll explain what it means and how to respond before the deadline.

Do you work with businesses as well as individuals?

Yes — individuals, professionals, proprietors, partnership firms and companies.

How do I book a consultation?
