FAQ

Common questions

Do I need an appointment?

Appointments help us keep waiting times short. Use the form above, call, or WhatsApp us and our team will confirm a slot.

Is root canal treatment painful?

The procedure is done under local anaesthesia, so most patients feel pressure rather than pain. It relieves the toothache that brought you in.

Am I a candidate for dental implants?

Most adults with good general health can get implants. A consultation and X-ray help the dentist check bone support and plan the right option.

Do you treat children?

Yes — we offer age-appropriate dental care for kids, from first check-ups to cavity treatment.

Will I know the cost before treatment?

Yes. Your dentist explains the plan and the treatment cost before starting.

Do you help patients coming from abroad?

Yes. We can plan your treatment schedule around your travel dates — contact us in advance.
