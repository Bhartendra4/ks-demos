Our little red barn

Scoops, smiles and summer evenings in the park.

Holy Cow! is a family-owned ice cream shop tucked inside Glen Maury Park, right by the ball fields. Stop by after a game, a walk or a day on the river for a generous hand-scooped cone, a thick milkshake or a hot burger — and ask what flavors are in the case today.
